    6 Export / Import

    CSV is UTF-8 (with BOM for Excel). Import accepts CSV (comma, semicolon or tab separated) and also .xlsx. Recognised columns: Lat/Latitude, Lon/Long/Lng/Longitude, Altitude/Elevation, District, Block, Village, Site (plus all columns this tool exports).

    Data definitions (important for research use)
    Map-selected locationLatitude/longitude of the place you clicked on the map. Positional accuracy depends on zoom level and basemap/imagery alignment. GPS Accuracy is recorded as “Map selected”.
    GPS locationLatitude/longitude reported by this device’s geolocation (GPS / Wi-Fi / cell). Its reported horizontal accuracy (m) is stored in “GPS Accuracy (m)”.
    Elevation (Altitude (m))Terrain height retrieved from an elevation service / DEM for the stored coordinates. The service used is recorded in “Elevation Source”.
    GPS altitudeHeight reported by the device GPS, if any. Stored separately in “GPS Altitude (m)” and never mixed with DEM elevation.
